Picky

The shopping list, beautifully simple.

Picky is a simple and beautiful shopping list app. It is designed to help you focus on shopping while minimizing the clutter and confusion of managing your lists.

Free · iPhone · iPad · Apple Watch

A list for every trip

Groceries, a party, a pie recipe: keep them apart. Tap and hold a list in the main menu to re-order it.

02

Aisles in color

Built-in aisles with icons and colors keep similar products together, and you can customize them.

03

Type less

About 1,600 built-in products, smart suggestions as you type, and search that forgives typos. Or browse everything in the Product Library.

04

Swipe, and it's bought

Swipe an item right to mark it as bought, or left to remove it. Pull the list up to clear all checked items.

05

A style for every list

Give each list its own theme, from Light Mix and North to Sunset and Ocean.

06

And a font to match

Default, Light or Heavy, monospaced or serif. Every list can look the way you like it.
